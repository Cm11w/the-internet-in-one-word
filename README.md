# The Internet in One Word

[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b/Cm11w/the-internet-in-one-word/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b)

One word for the whole internet — the funniest, most fitting word wins,
judged two at a time by a comedy-editor judge that weighs being one word
first, then funny, then true of the whole internet, then surprising, then
short.

## The word

**nobody**

Nobody is in charge of the internet. Nobody knows who anybody is.
Nobody asked — and everybody answered anyway.

## Why this word

Against the judge's brief, in its order:

1. **One word, on subject** — a single word describing the whole
   internet, not one app, one site, or one mood.
2. **Funny** — it is the internet's oldest joke: on the internet, nobody
   knows you're a dog. The laugh needs no explanation.
3. **Fits the whole internet** — true of every corner of it at once: no
   one is in charge, no one knows who anyone is, and "nobody asked" is
   the reply to everything.
4. **Surprising** — the field reaches for doomscrolling, brainrot,
   feral, unhinged and enshittification; nobody steps sideways from all
   of them.
5. **Lands in one beat** — six letters, instantly readable, and short
   enough to help on the size tie-break. The program also guards the
   word twice before writing: it must be one whitespace-free token of
   1–30 characters and plain lowercase letters, so the gate can never
   refuse the output.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the word to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
